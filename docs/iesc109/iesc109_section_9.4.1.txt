**Example 9.4** Mass of an object is 10 kg. What is its weight on the earth? 
## **Solution:** 
Mass, _m_ = 10 kg 
Acceleration due to gravity, _g_ = 9.8 m s[–2] _W = m_ × _g W_ = 10 kg × 9.8 m s[-2] = 98 N Thus, the weight of the object is 98 N.
