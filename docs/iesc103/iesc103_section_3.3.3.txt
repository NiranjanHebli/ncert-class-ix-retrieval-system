## **3.3.3 WHAT IS AN ION?** 
Compounds composed of metals and nonmetals contain charged species. The charged species are known as _ions_ . Ions may consist of a single charged atom or a group of atoms that have a net charge on them. An ion can be negatively or positively charged. A negatively charged ion is called an ‘anion’ and the positively charged ion, a ‘cation’. Take, for example, sodium chloride (NaCl).
