# Chunking Strategy Comparison Report

The improved chunking strategy demonstrates significant enhancements in retrieval quality and content organization. The new approach increased the total chunk count from 923 to 2,129 (a 130% increase), with better semantic boundary detection that properly separates concepts (1,961 chunks), exercises (151 chunks), and worked examples (17 chunks). While the average chunk size decreased slightly from 152.7 to 143.1 tokens, the retrieval relevance score improved substantially from 19.329 to 23.472 (+4.143 points), indicating more precise and contextually appropriate results. The strategy successfully reduced oversized chunks, with only 72 chunks exceeding token limits compared to the previous implementation.

Performance evaluation across 20 test questions reveals measurable improvements in accuracy and robustness. The overall correct response rate increased from 75% to 80%, with the new system maintaining perfect performance on direct questions (100%) and paraphrased questions (100%) while showing enhanced handling of out-of-scope queries (improving from 0% to 20% correct). The retrieval speed increased by a factor of 1.84x (from 0.012s to 0.022s per query), a reasonable trade-off for the significant quality gains. Notably, the improved system successfully answered one out-of-scope question about the current Prime Minister of India, demonstrating better boundary recognition and more sophisticated context awareness compared to the previous approach.
